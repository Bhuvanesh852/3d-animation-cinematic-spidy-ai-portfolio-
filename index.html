Assumptions and limits of this demo
  • The numbers are generated in your browser for this demo. They are not real financial data.
  • Net savings is total income minus total expenses for the filters you picked.
  • Expense ratio is total expenses divided by total income, rounded to a whole percent, and shows n/a when income is zero.
  • The filters are type (all, income, expenses) and month (the full year or one month).
  • The real project is the Financial Analytics Dashboard described in the case study above. This demo recreates its idea with generated numbers.
